# Open-Source Models

Open-source models are community-maintained AI systems that can be run locally or hosted by third parties.

## Strengths

- Transparency and customizability
- No vendor lock-in
- Good for experimentation and private deployments

## Best Uses

- Research and fine-tuning
- On-premise inference
- Niche or specialized tasks

## Tips

- Choose a model that matches your compute budget.
- Use prompt engineering to adapt general models.
- Monitor output quality carefully when using smaller models.
