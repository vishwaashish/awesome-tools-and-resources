# Gemini

Gemini is Google's family of AI models focused on multimodal understanding, reasoning, and productivity.

## Strengths

- Handles text and images well
- Good at complex reasoning
- Strong productivity and research support

## Best Uses

- Multimodal analysis
- Research summaries
- Code and design assistance

## Tips

- Use explicit prompts for mixed content.
- Give examples when asking for structured output.
- Ask for step-by-step reasoning for complex problems.
