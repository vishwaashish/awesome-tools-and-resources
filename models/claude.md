# Claude

Claude is Anthropic's family of models designed for safe and helpful AI assistance with an emphasis on context and alignment.

## Strengths

- Safety-oriented responses
- Good at long-form reasoning
- Useful for creative and analytical tasks

## Best Uses

- Research summarization
- Ethical reasoning
- Support for multi-turn tasks

## Tips

- Provide explicit context.
- Use high-level directives for analysis.
- Specify if you want concise or detailed output.
