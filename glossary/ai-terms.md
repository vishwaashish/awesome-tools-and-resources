# AI Terms

A quick glossary of common AI and prompt engineering terms.

## Terms

- **Agent**: An AI-driven workflow that can act on tasks or data.
- **Chain-of-thought**: A prompt style that asks the model to explain reasoning.
- **Fine-tuning**: Adapting a model to specific data or tasks.
- **Prompt Engineering**: Crafting prompts to get better AI outputs.
- **Tooling**: External capabilities used by AI agents, such as search or code execution.
