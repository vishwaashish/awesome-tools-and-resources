# Prompt Modes Guide

This page summarizes common prompt modes that help structure AI interactions. Use the quick reference for the top modes, then explore the full list for the right mode in each category.

## Top 10 Prompt Modes

1. **ELI5** – Explain a topic in simple terms.
2. **First Principles** – Break a problem down to its fundamentals.
3. **Socratic Method** – Learn through guided questions.
4. **Steelman** – Present the strongest version of an argument.
5. **Devil's Advocate** – Test an idea from the opposite perspective.
6. **Root Cause Analysis** – Identify the core cause of a problem.
7. **Five Whys** – Keep asking "why" until you reach the root cause.
8. **Tradeoff Analysis** – Compare benefits and drawbacks.
9. **Pre-Mortem** – Assume something fails and uncover reasons.
10. **Code Review** – Analyze code for quality and issues.

# Prompt Modes Reference

Use these modes to guide the model toward the reasoning style you need.

## Reasoning & Analysis

### First Principles
Separate a problem into basic building blocks and reconstruct the solution from there.

### Second-Order Thinking
Evaluate the consequences of your decisions and the consequences of those consequences.

### Bayesian Thinking
Update beliefs based on new evidence and observed outcomes.

### Systems Thinking
Consider how different parts of a system interact and influence one another.

### Root Cause Analysis
Find the underlying cause of an issue rather than just the symptoms.

### Five Whys
Ask "why" repeatedly to discover deeper reasons behind a result.

### Decision Matrix
Compare options using weighted criteria to choose the best path.

### Cost-Benefit Analysis
Weigh the costs and benefits of different alternatives.

### Tradeoff Analysis
Examine what is gained and what is sacrificed by each option.

### Risk Analysis
Identify potential risks and assess their likely impact.

### Pre-Mortem
Imagine a future failure and work backward to prevent it.

### Post-Mortem
Analyze what happened after an outcome, whether success or failure.

### OODA Loop
Apply Observe, Orient, Decide, Act to make faster, smarter decisions.

## Argument & Debate

### Steelman
Build the strongest possible version of an argument before evaluating it.

### Strawman
Create a weak or exaggerated version of an argument to expose flaws.

### Devil's Advocate
Challenge an idea by arguing the opposite position.

### Debate Mode
Present multiple viewpoints and compare them fairly.

### Cross-Examination
Question assumptions, evidence, and reasoning critically.

## Interaction & Personas

### Truth Mode
Ask the model to prioritize honesty and accuracy in its response.

### /human
Respond as if interacting directly with a human; keep tone natural and grounded.

### Red Team
Critically challenge the idea by attacking it from an adversarial perspective.

### Future You
Answer as if you are the future version of yourself, offering perspective from future experience.

## Teaching

### ELI5
Explain something as if to a 5-year-old: simple, clear, and easy to understand.

### ELI10
Explain as if to a 10-year-old: a little more detail, still accessible.

### Feynman Technique
Teach a concept by breaking it down into plain language and examples.

### Socratic Method
Guide learning through questions rather than direct answers.

## Writing

### TL;DR
Summarize the key points in a short, compact form.

### Executive Summary
Give a concise, business-ready overview of the most important ideas.

## Coding

### Code Review
Inspect code for correctness, clarity, and maintainability.

### Refactor
Improve structure, readability, and simplicity without changing behavior.

### Rubber Duck
Explain the problem step-by-step to discover issues and reasoning.

## Planning

### Project Manager
Focus on execution, timelines, and next actions.

### Chief of Staff
Focus on priorities, decision-making, and coordination.

## Creative

### Brainstorm
Generate many ideas quickly without judging them.

### Story Doctor
Improve narrative, structure, or emotional impact of a story.

### Worldbuilding
Create rich settings, rules, and background details for fiction or systems.
